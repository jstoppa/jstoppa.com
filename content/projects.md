## [Grok Bot looking for cursor](/grokbot/)

[![Grok Bot, a black circle with two white slanted eyes](/grokbot/card.png)](/grokbot/)

A little bot face built with three.js whose eyes follow your cursor around the screen. Move away and it stares at where you left, then gets confused and searches for you, then sad, then bored. Click to tickle it, and keep clicking to make it laugh.

**[Open Grok Bot →](/grokbot/)**

## [JSON Form online that actually works](/jsonforms/)

A JSON Form that actually works. Paste a JSON Schema, a UI schema and some data, and the form renders live as you type. It supports tabs, rules that show, hide, enable, disable or make fields read-only, and desktop, iPad and mobile layouts.

**[Open JSON Form Online →](/jsonforms/)**
